Welcome to my academic research website! I am a researcher specializing in computational neuroscience, neural dynamics, and cellular-level modeling. 

My work focuses on developing and simulating computational models to investigate neural mechanisms, membrane potentials, and synaptic plasticity.

## Research Focus

* **Computational Neuroscience:** Modeling single-cell and network-level neural dynamics.
* **Synaptic Plasticity:** Simulating spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) mechanisms.
* **Simulation Environments:** Developing custom mechanisms using Python, NEURON, and Spyder.
* **Neural Stimulation & Excitability:** Investigating axonal dynamics in response to external electric fields.
